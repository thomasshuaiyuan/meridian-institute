# Meridian Institute

Computational methods for pathogen evolution, biological risk quantification, and outbreak intelligence.

We build open, reproducible pipelines at the intersection of phylodynamics, genomic epidemiology, and evolutionary biology — focused on the risks that must be priced before a countermeasure reaches populations.

**Active work**

- **Evolutionary risk pricing:** Attenuation barriers, antigenic escape trajectories, and resistance emergence rates across deployed biological countermeasures — pre-registered comparative frameworks, not post-hoc analysis
- **Genomic observability census:** Quantifying the gap between WHO case reporting and public sequence deposition across 183 reporting units — the missing denominator in outbreak inference
- **Surveillance-based R(t) onset detection:** Cross-pathogen evaluation in Hong Kong 2014–2026
- **Spillover & emergence inference:** Phylodynamic early-warning for zoonotic host-range crossing at the H5N1 and GBA wildlife–livestock–human interface
