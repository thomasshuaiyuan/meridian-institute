## 1. PURPOSE

This Agreement governs a research collaboration in which Party B's laboratory will conduct wet-lab validation experiments designed to test predictions generated by Party A's Barrier Metric Framework for live-attenuated viral vaccines (LAVs). The goal is to generate experimental evidence supporting or refuting the framework's predictive power, suitable for co-authored publication.

---

## 2. SCOPE OF COLLABORATION

**Party A will:**
- Provide computational predictions (barrier scores, reversion probability estimates) for agreed-upon viral strains or constructs specified by mutual consent.
- Provide technical documentation sufficient for Party B to design appropriate experiments.
- Analyse experimental data provided by Party B and integrate findings into the barrier metric validation dataset.
- Lead manuscript preparation for papers primarily describing the barrier metric method.

**Party B will:**
- Design and conduct wet-lab experiments (e.g., serial passage, phenotypic assays, sequencing) to test barrier metric predictions, using strains and conditions agreed in advance.
- Provide all experimental data, quality-controlled and annotated, to Party A within [30] days of completion of each experimental block.
- Contribute to manuscript preparation as co-author on publications arising from this collaboration.

**Excluded from this Agreement:**
- Development of new algorithms, software, or computational methods (those remain solely Party A's).
- Sharing of any unpublished institutional IP not described in this Agreement without separate written consent.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 **Pre-existing IP.** Each party retains sole ownership of all IP created before or outside of this collaboration. Party A's Barrier Metric Framework, VC2 Methodology, and Reversion Probability Pipeline are Party A's pre-existing IP.

3.2 **Collaboration IP.** Any new IP arising directly from this collaboration (including jointly analysed datasets, jointly developed protocols, and jointly authored manuscripts) shall be owned by Party A, with a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual academic licence granted to Party B's laboratory for non-commercial research purposes.

3.3 **Commercial use.** Any commercial licensing of IP arising from this collaboration requires Party A's (or the Company's) written consent. Party B's laboratory will not commercially exploit results from this collaboration without Party A's prior written agreement.

3.4 **No transfer of Party A's core IP.** Nothing in this Agreement transfers or licenses Party A's pre-existing IP beyond what is strictly necessary for the validation experiments described herein.
