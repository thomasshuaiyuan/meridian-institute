Research Programme

Turning “it will stay attenuated”
into a number.

Shuai (Thomas) Yuan · Founder
Doctoral researcher, HKU-Pasteur Research Pole · Dhanasekaran Laboratory
Draft v1 · November 2026

Meridian exists to turn will this attenuated vaccine stay attenuated from a regulatory assertion into a phylodynamic estimand — and to monitor the answer in the field at population scale.

01 — The thesis

AI collapses the cost of analysis. It does not compress evolutionary time.

Design-space search, inference under bias, statistical-strength transfer across settings — these are compute-bound and they are shrinking fast. What does not shrink is evolutionary time, data-generation time, and institutional time. The incompressible residue is the quantification of evolutionary risk in biological countermeasures, and the measurement systems that make such quantification trustworthy.

Health intelligence scales with wealth. The largest outbreaks occur in the least-resourced settings and are the least observed — measurably so: surveillance effort scales sublinearly with epidemic size. Live-attenuated vaccines carry evolutionary risk that is priced nowhere before deployment. Antigenic drift erodes vaccine-matched immunity on timescales no trial can follow. No model can retrospectively surveil the unsampled.

Meridian works at that boundary: pre-registered evolutionary-risk frameworks, observation-bias quantification, and immune-landscape reconstruction — the layer between a model’s output and the claim that has to survive regulatory or peer scrutiny.

The operating standard follows from it. Every analysis pre-registered, every claim traced to an artifact, every correction numbered and public. When analysis is cheap, credibility is the scarce asset, and pre-registration is how it compounds.

03 — Portfolio

One flagship. One second application. Two gated directions.

The framework is the programme; the pathogens are demonstrations of it. No parallel launches.

Flagship

Reversion — attenuation stability as a measured quantity

The question. Will an engineered live-attenuated vaccine stay attenuated — through manufacturing passage, inside a vaccinee, and along a transmission chain? Today this is answered by assertion, backed by passage studies in cell substrates that do not resemble the deployment environment.

The current standard, and where it stops.

  • WHO requires genetic stability demonstrated to a passage level comparable to final vaccine bulk and preferably beyond the anticipated maximum, with acceptable limits for variation defined by the manufacturer and agreed with the national regulatory authority — that is, ad hoc, per product, with no underlying evolutionary model [1].
  • FDA/CBER requires full seed sequencing, subpopulation analysis, and genetic stability including susceptibility to reversion — but prescribes no framework for computing it. It notes explicitly that different cell substrates impose different selective pressures and different reversion likelihoods, the Sabin poliovirus precedent [2].
  • WHO’s live influenza recommendations state the field’s core empirical finding in qualitative language only: a single temperature-sensitive attenuating mutation can be expected to revert even after one passage in human recipients, while multi-segment attenuation appears to contribute to stability [3]. No quantitative multi-locus reversion model exists.
  • High-throughput sequencing is now expected, but there is no statistical framework for reading minority-variant frequency spectra as reversion-potential evidence — and consensus sequencing discards exactly that information [1].
  • Post-marketing monitoring is a slogan — “monitored carefully during the post-marketing period” [3] — with no defined estimand, sampling design, or decision threshold. Polio’s vaccine-derived poliovirus system is the only working template, and it is manual and pathogen-specific. Mutation-specific reversion quantification exists in bespoke per-product assays [4], not as a method.

What Meridian adds — three pillars.

  1. Design-time: a reversion-potential estimand. Genome-scale conservation across a clinical genome corpus, fitness-landscape priors and deep-mutational-scanning integration, combined into a quantitative reversion-barrier score for any engineered deletion — with the polio VDPV record as validation ground truth. Founding analysis in HSV-2, ranking five published vaccine constructs.
  2. Deployment-time: phylodynamics of vaccine-derived lineages. Genomic surveillance machinery — genomic R(t), lineage turnover, delay-aware correction — extended to detect and monitor vaccine-lineage circulation, reversion and reassortment in field sequences, and to distinguish vaccine-lineage transmission from importation.
  3. System-time: signal trust. Specification-curve and observation-bias machinery as the statistical backbone: how much genomic signal, over what window, before “this vaccine lineage is evolving” becomes a claim a regulator can act on.

The hook. WHO and FDA documents both invite manufacturer-proposed quantitative criteria [1][2]. Nobody has proposed a principled one. The target output is a reversion-risk score admissible in a pre-regulatory dossier.

Second application

Escape — antigenic escape trajectories and strain selection

The same machinery run in the opposite direction: not where the vaccine virus cannot go, but where the wild virus cannot hide. Genomic R(t), lineage turnover and conservation analysis become an escape-potential estimator for strain-update decisions — influenza composition, variant-updated vaccines — which today run largely on antigenic-distance heuristics. The lane connects to WHO vaccine-composition networks and is the natural second application of the flagship’s methods. It is also more crowded, which is why it is sequenced second rather than first.

Direction — gated

Resistance — antimicrobial resistance emergence

Resistance climbs measurable evolutionary trajectories before clinical emergence. Phylodynamic estimation applied to AMR is underdeveloped, and generalising the framework there would test whether it is a framework at all or only a vaccine method. It is a different community, a different funder base and a different data infrastructure. Listed as a direction, not a commitment. No Meridian output.

Direction — gated

Spillover — phylodynamic early warning at the host-range crossing

HKU-Pasteur’s position at the H5N1 and Greater Bay Area interface is the asset. The prerequisites are surveillance access at the animal–human interface and a delay-aware inference stack validated on influenza first. Listed honestly as planned, with no output yet.

The sequencing rule. The framework is the career; the modalities are demonstrations of it. One flagship, one second application, two gated directions.

06 — Conflicts and standards

The standard, applied here first.

Conflicts of interest. The founder holds a computational biology role at Hyper Biotech (Shenzhen), a company with a live-attenuated vaccine programme. Hyper Biotech has no role in, and provides no funding for, Meridian outputs. Construct rankings published by Meridian include competitors of any Hyper Biotech programme, and the ranking method is pre-registered before results are known so that the ordering cannot be chosen after the fact. Use of HKU data is governed by university policy.

Inventorship. The founder is a named inventor on Chinese invention-patent application 202610917181.7, applicant Shenzhen Haibo Biotechnology Co., Ltd., “一种单纯疱疹病毒2型减毒株及其构建方法和应用” (a herpes simplex virus type 2 attenuated strain, its construction method and application), published 28 July 2026, not granted. That application covers HB01, a live-attenuated HSV-2 construct in the same class as those Meridian’s reversion-barrier analysis compares. HB01 is held out of the published comparison; the comparator set is RVx201, HSV-529, 0ΔRING and VC2, all developed by others. This is a direct interest in the object of the measurement rather than a general affiliation, and it is disclosed here, in the preprint, and in any dossier the score enters. The mitigation is structural rather than declarative: the founder’s own construct is excluded from the ranking, and the method and its acceptance criteria are registered before results exist.

Pre-registration. Comparative analyses commit to their estimand, their comparator set and their acceptance criteria before the result exists. Where a pre-registered prediction fails, the failure is reported with the same prominence as a success.

Provenance. Quantitative claims trace to a logged, regenerable analysis. A result that cannot be regenerated does not enter the record.

Corrections. Numbered, dated and public. A correction is not an embarrassment; an uncorrected record is.
