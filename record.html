What I said, and what became of it.

Every forward-looking claim this programme makes is filed here with a date it resolves by. When that date arrives the claim is marked met or missed, and a miss is recorded with the same prominence as anything else. Corrections to published pages are logged below them.

This exists because the alternative is a programme that describes itself as rigorous and has no mechanism that could ever show otherwise. The doctrine explains why the mechanism is the claim.

How this page works

Dates do not move and criteria are not restated. A claim whose deadline slides or whose conditions get rewritten after filing is not a prediction. It is a mission statement with a calendar attached.

A miss is published at the same size as a hit. Nothing here is quietly deleted; a superseded entry stays with its replacement linked.

At least one scored miss per year. If a year passes with none, the thing to doubt is not the accuracy — it is whether the scoring is real.

Corrections are a success of the system. They are logged against the page that changed, dated, and never folded silently into a revision.

Documents are numbered and dated and are superseded rather than edited. This page is the index of what has been superseded, corrected, or withdrawn.
